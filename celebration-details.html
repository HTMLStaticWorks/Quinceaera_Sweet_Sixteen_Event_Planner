Event Production Case Study

Valeria's Royal Emerald Quinceañera

A regal fusion of traditional Latin heritage, deep emerald velvet textures, crystal chandelier lighting, and a 16-member Court of Honor grand waltz.

Venue The Grand Biltmore Ballroom
Guest Count 240 Guests
Planning Scope The Signature Experience
Theme Royal Emerald & Brushed Gold
The Vision

Honoring Heritage with Royal Splendor

When Valeria and her family first met with our creative team, they envisioned an atmosphere that felt undeniably majestic yet deeply welcoming to four generations of family members. Valeria had selected an intricate, hand-beaded emerald ballgown with gold filigree lace, inspiring the entire scenography palette.

Our studio served as executive producers across 11 months of development: securing The Biltmore Ballroom, directing three rigorous Court of Honor rehearsals, orchestrating church ceremony protocols, and building a 40-foot floral stage with personalized monogram projections.

Celebration Photo Highlights

Our Production Approach

1. The Court of Honor Direction

14 chambelanes and damas were rehearsed across three studio workshops, synchronizing their ceremonial entrance, bow sequence, and high-energy surprise dance.

2. Scenography & Lightcraft

We installed 32 wireless uplights calibrated to royal emerald, paired with automated pin-spotting on every floral centerpiece and a 16-foot custom gold gloss dance floor.

3. Emotional Traditions Preserved

The changing of the shoes, the crowning tiara blessing by her abuela, and the emotional father-daughter waltz were staged with tender, unhurried reverence.
