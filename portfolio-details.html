Case Study Feature

Camila's Midnight Glam Sweet Sixteen

A high-octane celebration staged at The Obsidian Glasshouse featuring custom illuminated neon staging, a runway dress reveal, and concert sound engineering.

Celebration Type Sweet Sixteen
Venue The Obsidian Glasshouse Pavilion
Theme Celestial Midnight Glam
Guest Count 190 Guests
The Concept

From Met Gala Inspiration to Live Gala

Camila wanted her sixteenth birthday to feel like an exclusive New York Fashion Week afterparty. Moving away from traditional ballroom clichés, our design studio transformed an industrial glass conservatory with 200 feet of black velvet drapery, crystal bead chandeliers, and a custom glossy black runway leading to the dance floor.

When Camila stepped onto the runway for her grand reveal, a synchronized 4-point spotlight tracked her entrance while synchronized cold-spark fountains fired, creating an indelible opening moment.

Event Photography Collection

Production Highlights

1. The 16-Candle Ceremony

A customized illuminated candelabra with individual music stems for each of the 16 dedications, keeping the ceremony engaging and emotionally resonant.

2. Molecular Mocktail Bar

A bespoke glowing bar station offering dry-ice smoking mocktails, glitter cotton candy toppers, and personalized glassware with Camila's monogram.

3. 360 Video Booth & Neon Lounge

A dedicated social media lounge with a 360-degree slow motion video platform and custom neon quote signage that generated over 4,000 views within 24 hours.
